# PiPy Community Code of Conduct

PiPy is an open technical project. Participation should make it easier for people to contribute useful work, ask questions, challenge designs, and learn from mistakes.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- treat other people with respect;
- critique ideas, code, measurements, and designs rather than people;
- state uncertainty when evidence is incomplete;
- give technical disagreement room to be direct without becoming personal;
- assume good faith while still asking for evidence;
- make space for contributors with different levels of experience;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- follow responsible disclosure practices for security issues;
- accept maintainer decisions about project scope even when proposing alternatives.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation;
- discriminatory or demeaning language directed at a person or group;
- sexualized attention or imagery directed at participants;
- deliberate outing, doxxing, or publication of private information;
- sustained personal attacks;
- disruption whose primary purpose is preventing productive project activity;
- knowingly publishing undisclosed security vulnerabilities before maintainers have a reasonable opportunity to respond.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is permitted. PiPy deals with distributed systems, networking, cryptography, and hardware, where design choices have real tradeoffs.

It is acceptable to say that an approach is incorrect, unsafe, inefficient, insufficiently tested, or architecturally inconsistent. Explain why and support the claim with reasoning, tests, references, measurements, or a reproducible failure when possible.

It is not acceptable to turn technical criticism into attacks on competence, identity, motives, or worth.

## Scope

This code applies to PiPy repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, review threads, and project-run community spaces. It also applies when someone is clearly representing the project in another public space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation when behavior materially violates this code.

For a conduct concern involving another contributor, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub rather than escalating the dispute in the thread. If the concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting mechanisms where appropriate.

Enforcement decisions should prioritize participant safety, project continuity, proportionality, and a clear record of the behavior being addressed.
